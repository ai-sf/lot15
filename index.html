Lights of Tuscany

Thank you for participating!


Who

About 40 physics students from all levels of study are expected to participate, 2/3 of whom international. They must be IAPS members.

When

In December 2015, from Thursday 17th to Monday 21st.

Where

In Tuscany. Arrival, accommodation and departure are expected to be in Pisa. A couple of days will be spent in Florence, both for science related activities and sightseeing.

An event part of the celebrations for the International Year of Light.

Careers in Photonics Light Talk

After the Opening Ceremony there will be a LIGHTalk consisting of an array of live presenters from the scientific, enterpreneurial and industry communities speaking about different aspects of photonics. It will be open to the general public.

Associazione Italiana Studenti di Fisica

The Associazione Italiana Studenti di Fisica (AISF) gathers together all the Italian students of physics. It was founded in Heidelberg in August 2014 and since January 2015 is a National Committee for the International Associazion of Physics Students (IAPS).
